LIMITLESS

THE AWAKENING
He awoke without a name.
Without a past.
Only fragments remained.

CHAPTER ONE

Darkness.

A breath.

His eyes opened.

Nothing felt familiar.

Then—

A memory.

A voice.

He couldn't see the face.

He couldn't remember the name.

But he remembered the words.

"You have to remember."

The memory remained.

And for the first time, he knew one thing:

Someone wanted him to remember.
