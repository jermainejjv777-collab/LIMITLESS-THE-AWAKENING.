Chapter One

The Awakening

He woke before the alarm.

For a few seconds, he didn't move. He simply stared into the darkness, listening to the quiet rhythm of his own breathing.

Something felt different.

Not wrong.

Just... familiar.

A place he couldn't name.
A voice he couldn't remember.
A feeling that somehow belonged to him.

He closed his eyes.

The memory didn't disappear.

It remained.

Somewhere beneath the thoughts he could consciously reach, something was beginning to surface.

He opened his eyes again.

His surroundings looked exactly the same.

But his body didn't feel the same.

His breathing had changed. His balance felt different. Even the way his hands rested against the surface beneath him felt strangely natural.

His mind remembered a fragment.

His body remembered something else.

He stood slowly.

There was no sudden transformation. No explanation. No voice telling him what was happening.

Only the quiet realization that whatever had been asleep inside him was no longer completely asleep.

He didn't know who he had been.

But some part of him already knew that he had been someone.

He looked toward the window.

The morning waited outside.

And for the first time in a long time, he felt like he was supposed to be somewhere.

REMEMBER

The memories had not vanished.

They were waiting.

And now, slowly, so was the rest of him.
